Product Options

Option meanings Featured Product: Marks this item for extra visibility in hand-picked sections such as homepage highlights, promotional collections, staff picks, or campaign-driven product groups. It is mainly a merchandising flag used to showcase important products. Universal Fit: Use this when the product works across many vehicles or applications and does not depend on a strict fitment map. It helps prevent the item from being treated as compatible with only one brand, model, or year range. Requires Serial: Enable this when each unit must be tracked by serial number, batch number, or another unique identifier during receiving, storage, fulfillment, warranty handling, or after-sales support. Returnable: Indicates whether customers can send the product back through the normal returns process after delivery. Turn this off for final-sale, special-order, hazardous, installed, or otherwise non-returnable items.

Operations & Policies

Professional product operations Internal Reference Code: Your company’s internal stock or sourcing code for this item. Use it for warehouse operations, catalog matching, and internal reporting. Stock Strategy: Tells your team whether the item is kept in stock, handled as a special order, or produced/prepared only when needed. Min Order Qty: The smallest quantity your company should sell or process in one order line. Return Policy: Professional catalogs define both eligibility and customer-facing return terms, not only a yes/no toggle. Serial Tracking Note: Internal instructions for capturing serial, IMEI, batch, or lot numbers during fulfillment and support. Fulfillment Note: Extra handling instructions for packing, dispatch, installation, hazmat checks, or warehouse coordination.

Pricing & Inventory

Field meanings Selling Price: The live customer-facing price. Compare At: The higher reference price used for discounts or strike-through pricing. Unit Cost: Your internal cost for one unit. Inventory Source: The internal warehouse, stock source, or operational origin used by your company for this item. Stock Status: Current availability state shown for the product. On Hand Qty: The actual inventory count available now. Safety Stock: Minimum quantity buffer before the item is considered low stock. Lead Time: Estimated number of days needed to restock or fulfill. Warehouse Bin: Internal storage location for picking the item. Warranty: Coverage period or warranty terms for the product. Shipping Class: Fulfillment/shipping category used for handling rules.

Fitment, Contents & SEO

Section meanings Vehicle Fitment: Defines which vehicles, trims, engines, or year ranges this product is compatible with. This is important for catalog accuracy, product search, and reducing incorrect orders. Package Contents: Lists exactly what the buyer receives in the box, such as the main part, hardware, brackets, seals, cables, or instruction sheets. Tags: Short merchandising or search-oriented labels that help group products internally or surface them in promotional experiences. Notes: Internal-only operational notes for QA, purchasing, packing, support, or merchandising teams. SEO Title: Search-facing title meant to improve how the product appears in search engine results and browser tabs. SEO Description: Meta description summary used to improve search-result snippets and click-through quality.
